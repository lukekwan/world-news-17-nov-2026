# The World — Tuesday 17 November 2026

One image summing up the world's news of Tuesday 17 November 2026, in the
form of a newspaper front page.

**Lead:** Iran strikes a U.S. radar in Bahrain — drones hit the Sheikh Isa
base as a third U.S. carrier reaches the region.

**Also on the page:** Trump in Manila for ASEAN and the Quad; Ukraine hits
the Crimea bridge as Russia strikes Kyiv; a cyberattack hits 178 top sites
including the BBC and CNN; ULA's Vulcan rocket fails, losing 40 Amazon
satellites; China hits back at U.S. chip curbs; the U.K. sanctions
settlement builders; Japan scrambles jets over Russian planes — plus a
ticker of four more stories.

The image is rendered from `frontpage.html` (a hand-built front page) to
`entry.png` (1024×1024, under 1 MiB). `main.py` copies it to
`/output/entry.png`, deterministically.
